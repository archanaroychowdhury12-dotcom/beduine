Quick FAQ

Do I get discount credit immediately after buying a plan?

No. It is unlocked only as per the official winner/non-winner benefit rules.

What is TRC?

TRC means Travel Reward Credit for travel reward selection participation.

How does Travel Reward work?

Eligible members participate in the weekly digital selection process.

What happens if I don’t win?

You remain eligible for applicable member benefits as per company policy.

How can I use Discount Credit?

It can be applied during eligible paid tour booking.

Can I withdraw Discount Credit as cash?

No. It is a non-cash travel benefit.

Can I transfer my credit?

No. Credits remain linked to the member UID/account.

How do I book a paid tour?

Use the paid tour page or contact the support team for booking assistance.

Where can I see my UID?

After login, your UID is visible inside the member dashboard.
