Frequently Asked Questions

Who can buy a subscription?
Individuals aged 18 years or above.
How long is the subscription valid?
12 months from activation.
Is the subscription refundable?
No, subscription fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
Can I exchange winner benefits for cash?
No, selected-member tour benefits cannot be exchanged for cash or other packages.
What is 1 Discount Credit?
1 Domestic Discount Credit = ₹500 off for 1 person on an eligible Domestic paid tour. 1 International Discount Credit = ₹5,000 off for 1 person on an eligible International paid tour.
Can I use International Discount Credit on Domestic tours?
No. International DC can only be used for International bookings. Domestic DC can only be used for Domestic bookings. Domestic and International credits cannot be cross-used.
