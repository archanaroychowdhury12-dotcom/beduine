Can I use multiple Discount Credits on a single tour?
You can use 1 Discount Credit (₹500 off) per tour booking, allowing you to save money across multiple journeys throughout your 12-month subscription window.
Do these Discount Credits expire?
No, your credits are valid for the entire 12-month duration of your travel club membership.
